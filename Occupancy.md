# What is Occupancy Rate?

## Simple Answer

For **each unit**, occupancy rate is:
- **How many days this unit was occupied this year ÷ Total days in year**

That's it! It's calculated individually for each unit.

## Example

**Unit A:**
- Was rented for **80 days** this year
- Total days in year: **365 days**
- **Occupancy Rate = 80 ÷ 365 = 21.9%**

**Unit B:**
- Was rented for **0 days** this year
- Total days in year: **365 days**
- **Occupancy Rate = 0 ÷ 365 = 0%**

## Overall Occupancy Rate

The overall occupancy rate is simply the **average** of all individual unit occupancy rates.

**Example:**
- Unit A: 21.9%
- Unit B: 0%
- Unit C: 50%
- **Overall = (21.9 + 0 + 50) ÷ 3 = 23.97%**

## Why is it low?

A low percentage means:
- That specific unit was empty most of the year
- The unit needs more tenants or longer leases

## How to improve it?

- Get more tenants for that unit
- Get longer lease terms
- Reduce vacancy periods between tenants
